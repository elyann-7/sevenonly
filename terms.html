    1. Presentation of the Site

    This website is dedicated exclusively to presenting the SEVEN brand, which specialises in high-quality smash burgers. Our aim is to share our passion with our visitors by showing our products, our ingredients and where to find us. The Site informs the public about our story, our values and what makes our burgers special.

    2. Services Offered

    The Site gives users access to information including:

    • Our Menu, with presentations of our burgers, sides, sauces and desserts;
    • Our Story, about who we are and what we stand for;

    • Franchise, information for partners who want to bring SEVEN to their city;
