    OUR STORY

    Seven started with a simple belief: if we wouldn’t serve it to our own family, we wouldn’t serve it to you.

    We created Seven for the people we care about most, our families, our friends, and our community. That belief shapes everything we do, from the ingredients we choose and the freshness of our food to the cleanliness of our kitchens and the way we welcome every guest.

    We obsess over the details because the details matter. Every meal is prepared with care, consistency, and uncompromising standards, the same way we would proudly serve it at our own table.

    But Seven was never meant to be just another burger place. We wanted to create a place people look forward to visiting, coming back to, and sharing with the people they love.

    That’s Seven. Crafted to Obsess.
