    COOKIE POLICY

    SEVEN SMASH BURGER · GRAND BOULEVARD – NERGIS PARK, SULAYMANIYAH

    Publisher: SEVEN Smash Burger · info@sevenonly.com

    1. Use of Cookies

    A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser on your computer, phone or tablet, and which can be read again on a later visit.

    This website does not set any cookies of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not build profiles of visitors.

    2. Consent

    Because the Site does not use tracking or advertising cookies, it does not show a cookie banner. If we ever add such cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

    3. Types of Storage Used and Their Purposes

    Functional storage (no cookies): to work smoothly, the Site saves a few small technical notes in your browser’s own storage. They stay on your device, are never sent to us and do not identify you:

    • Intro shown: remembers that the opening animation already played during this visit (deleted when you close the tab);
    • Last visit time: the time you last left the Site, used only to decide whether to show the opening animation again;
    • Photo slider position: remembers which photo you were viewing during this visit (deleted when you close the tab).

    Audience measurement, tracking and advertising cookies: none are used.

    Third-party cookies from embedded content: see the list below.

    Social media: our Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube icons are simple links. No social-network code is loaded on the Site until you click one and leave for that network.

    3 bis. List of Third-Party Services

    Hosting (GitHub Pages): the Site is served by GitHub, which may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

    Google Maps (location section): the map is loaded from Google. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies and receive technical information such as your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.

    YouTube (playlist, only if a YouTube playlist is added): the player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only loads when you choose to play.

    4. Your Rights Regarding Your Personal Data

    You may ask us to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to info@sevenonly.com. For cookies set by Google, please use the tools and contacts in Google’s own privacy policy.

    5. Blocking and Deleting Cookies

    You can block and delete cookies and stored site data at any time in your browser settings, and choose to be warned before a cookie is stored. Blocking Google’s cookies may stop the map from working; the rest of the Site will still work. Instructions for the main browsers:

    • Google Chrome
    • Safari
    • Firefox
    • Microsoft Edge

    6. Embedded Content and Unforeseen Cookies

    Some content on the Site, such as the Google map, is hosted by other companies and shown on our Site. These companies may use cookies, and we do not control how they use them. If you find a cookie on our Site that is not described here, please let us know by email.

    7. Notes

    We will update this policy whenever the Site or the rules change. Please check this page for the latest version. For any question, contact us at info@sevenonly.com.
